About Me

I am a Senior Engineer at Huawei (2012 Labs), working on embodied intelligence and robot learning. I received dual Ph.D. degrees in 2025 through a joint doctoral program between City University of Hong Kong and Tianjin University, advised by Prof. Junhui Hou and Prof. Xiaodong Chen. Before that, I received my B.Eng. from Tianjin University in 2018.

My research lies at the intersection of geometry processing, computer graphics, and embodied intelligence, with a focus on implicit and differentiable geometry representations and their applications in 3D reconstruction, compression, and robotic systems.
