About Me

I am Siyu, currently working at Huawei, where I focus on research and development in Embodied Intelligence. I received my Ph.D. through a joint doctoral program between Tianjin University and City University of Hong Kong. During my doctoral studies at Tianjin University, I have had the privilege of joining Prof. Junhui Hou's research group at City University of Hong Kong, where I have been able to deepen my knowledge and contribute to cutting-edge research. My research interests lie in Geometry Processing, Computer Graphics, and Embodied Intelligence, with a particular focus on Implicit Geometry Representation and its applications in intelligent robotic systems.

Before that, I received my Bachelor's degree from Tianjin University in 2018. During my studies, I have published multiple papers in top-tier conferences and journals.
